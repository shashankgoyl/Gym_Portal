# Exercise media research note

Search results reviewed on 2026-08-20 included fitness-animation previews from Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Vecteezy, and YouTube. The available search results were stock or platform previews and were not incorporated into HP because their reuse terms are not established for this project.

HP therefore uses concrete external instructional demonstration links rather than copying third-party GIF assets. This preserves a direct form-reference path for each exercise while avoiding unverified asset licensing.
